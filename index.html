How this list is maintained. Every entry was checked against the provider's own pages on 12 November 2026: the cost, the hours, whether a certificate is awarded, and for certifications the live exam code. Where a provider's own marketing disagreed with its terms, the terms won.

Nothing here is an affiliate link. 100 of the 133 courses are free, which makes most of this list worth nothing in commission. That is rather the point: the ranking is not for sale.

University module lists were read off each institution's own course pages where marked verified. Where a programme is marked unverified, it exists and the subject coverage is an informed estimate rather than a checked module mapping, usually because the module list is not published in English. Universities revise modules every year.

Prices change and courses retire. Confirm before you pay. If you find something out of date, that is a bug worth reporting.
